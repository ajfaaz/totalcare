Hospital Operational Guide & FAQ
6 Modules

Receptionists and nurses can register new patients by clicking Register Patient from the left sidebar navigation.

  • Patient Photos: Upload a photo thumbnail during registration for quick verification.
  • EMR Folder: Access complete patient visit history, medical notes, lab results, and radiology scans in the unified EMR view.
  • Walk-in Visits: Easily create walk-in appointments for emergency or non-booked triage.

The Accountant Dashboard focuses on cash-paying self-sponsor patients to optimize daily revenue streams.

  • Generate Invoices: Add consultation, lab, radiology, and pharmacy items directly to patient bills.
  • Record Receipts: Capture full or partial cash payments, print official PDF invoices, and view real-time daily revenue reports.

For hospitals working with government health schemes (NHIS / KSCHMA) or private HMOs:

  • Assign coverage percentages to patients (e.g. 90% HMO, 10% Patient Co-pay).
  • Submit and track claim authorization codes under the NHIS / KSCHMA Claims section.

TotalCare monitors patient vital signs automatically. When abnormal vitals are recorded by a nurse:

  • An automated SLA alert is sent to the assigned doctor.
  • If unacknowledged within the SLA window, the system escalates the alert to Head Doctor and Hospital Admin.
  • Admins can monitor live response timers on the Vital Alerts Control Center.

Pharmacists manage stock levels and dispense doctor prescriptions seamlessly:

  • Stock levels automatically deduct upon medication dispensing.
  • Low stock warnings alert pharmacists before medications run out.
  • Audit logs record all stock adjustments and dispensing transactions.

Hospital Admins can manage staff accounts from the Admin Dashboard:

  • Create staff accounts with specific roles (Doctor, Nurse, Pharmacist, Lab, Radiologist, Accountant, Receptionist).
  • Reset forgotten staff passwords directly using the built-in password reset tool.
